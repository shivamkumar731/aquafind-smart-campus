ABOUT AQUAFIND

Designed around
everyday campus needs.

AquaFind is a student project concept developed for INT335 Design Thinking, project P16: The Empty Bottle Problem. It explores how students can find drinking-water points more easily and how facility issues can become more visible.

The concept is intended to be refined through real campus observations, interviews and user testing. It does not certify water quality or replace official campus maintenance channels.
